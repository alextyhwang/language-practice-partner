# Language Practice Partner

Language Practice Partner is a Duolingo-inspired speaking coach for real conversation practice. It uses OpenAI Realtime voice sessions to run structured roleplay missions, give live pronunciation and grammar feedback, and help learners retry the exact moments they struggle with.

The goal is to feel different from an open-ended AI voice chat: users choose a target language, scenario, and coaching mode, then complete short practice sessions with corrections, scoring, streaks, and progress over time.

## Coaching Modes

- Strict teacher: frequent corrections, pronunciation drills, and repeat-until-clear practice.
- Friendly travel buddy: natural roleplay with gentle feedback and confidence-building recaps.
- Interviewer: realistic follow-up questions with feedback on clarity, structure, and fluency.

## Early Product Direction

- Realtime speech-to-speech practice with WebRTC.
- Scenario-based missions for travel, daily life, interviews, and exams.
- Live correction cards for pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary, and fluency.
- End-of-session recap with targeted drills for the next practice round.
